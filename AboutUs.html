About Raja Farms

Raja Farms is a Rural Mart dedicated to empowering rural farmers and strengthening the dairy ecosystem. We work at the grassroots level to ensure that small and medium dairy farmers have access to the right knowledge, technology, and resources needed to build sustainable and profitable dairy operations.

We serve as a one-stop solution connecting farmers, dairy entrepreneurs, and dairy companies with essential products and technical support required for modern dairy farming.

What We Do

At Raja Farms, we provide a complete range of dairy and farm support solutions, including:

  • Dairy milking equipment and technical support
  • Silage and fodder solutions for better cattle nutrition
  • High-quality fodder seeds
  • Ethno-veterinary and cattle healthcare products
  • Climate-friendly farming solutions
  • Farmer education and technical guidance
  • End-to-end support from small farmers to dairy companies

Our Purpose

We believe that strong farmers build a strong nation. Many rural farmers lack access to modern equipment, reliable inputs, and technical guidance.

Raja Farms bridges this gap by bringing affordable technology, trusted products, and practical knowledge directly to the farming community.

Our Vision

To become a trusted partner for rural dairy farmers by creating a sustainable, technology-enabled, and climate-responsible dairy ecosystem.

Our Mission

To improve farmer livelihoods by making dairy farming more efficient, profitable, and environmentally sustainable through education, innovation, and reliable supply of farm solutions.
